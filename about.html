Welcome to Avengers Database

Avengers Database is an independent fan-made website created to help visitors explore superhero characters, their stories, abilities, affiliations, and related fictional-universe information in one place.

Our goal is to make character information easy to browse on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. We aim to keep the website organized, readable, and useful for fans who enjoy learning about superheroes and their fictional worlds.

What you can find here

  • Character profiles and basic background information.
  • Information about abilities, teams, and fictional story connections.
  • A searchable character directory designed for easy browsing.
  • Updates and improvements to the website experience over time.

Independence and trademarks

Avengers Database is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Marvel Entertainment, The Walt Disney Company, or any related companies. Names of characters, franchises, and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

We aim to respect intellectual-property rights. If you are a rights holder and have a concern about content on this website, please contact us so we can review it.
